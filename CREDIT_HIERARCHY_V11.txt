V11 - CREDIT HIERARCHY RULES

CENTER: UNLIMITED source; can credit direct Operators only; no debit of Center wallet.
OPERATOR: can credit direct Admin children only; must have enough available balance.
ADMIN: can credit direct Supermanager children only; must have enough available balance.
SUPERMANAGER: can credit direct Agent children only; must have enough available balance.
AGENT: can credit direct User children only; must have enough available balance.
USER: cannot credit anyone.

The UI exposes “Shto kredit” only inside Lista e Përdoruesve for the direct child roles.
