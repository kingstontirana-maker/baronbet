MY BETS UPDATE - v19

This version updates ONLY the My Bets frontend in public/euro07.bet/vbet/user/build/main.js
and main.css. The server and settlement logic from v15 are preserved.

IMPORTANT:
- sportsbook.db is intentionally NOT included in this ZIP.

- This prevents your real tickets from being overwritten.
- My Bets now loads without a default date restriction, so existing tickets appear.
- Date presets and status filters are functional.
- Clicking a ticket expands its details.
