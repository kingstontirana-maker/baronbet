BET SLIP UPDATE – 2026-09-12

Implemented from the supplied desktop/mobile reference screenshots:

1. Desktop
- Bet Slip is now a fixed-height right-side panel with the light/grey visual treatment from the reference.
- Selection count is shown in a green circular badge.
- Header uses "Skedina" and keeps My Bets available.
- Selected bets use compact grey cards with yellow odds.
- Footer/inputs/buttons use the reference grey/green treatment.

2. Mobile / tablet
- The full Bet Slip is hidden by default.
- It appears only when at least one odd is selected.
- A fixed bottom bar shows: selection count, "Skedina", and total odds.
- Tapping the bar opens the Bet Slip as a bottom sheet.
- A dark backdrop and close button are provided while the sheet is open.
- Removing the last selection automatically closes the mobile sheet and hides the bottom bar.
- My Bets can also open the mobile sheet.

3. Existing betting logic preserved
- Selection/toggle behavior
- One selection per event
- Total odds calculation
- Stake and potential win calculation
- Place Bet flow
- Authentication and My Bets

Start with:
  npm start

Then open:
  http://localhost:3000/
